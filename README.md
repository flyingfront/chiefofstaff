# chiefofstaff
Chief of Staff
A personal Chief of Staff that runs inside Claude. Getting started takes about ten
minutes, and you don't need any AI experience.
Created by Garrett Lommatsch · www.lommatsch.com · linkedin.com/in/garrett-lommatsch

What is this?
Chief of Staff (CoS) turns Claude into the person every executive wishes they had: someone who
keeps the full picture of your work, watches your calendar and inbox, remembers what you said you'd
do, and tells you each morning what deserves your attention. It works for job searches, side projects,
content goals, family logistics, or just surviving a busy career.
It is direct by design. If you said you'd do something and didn't, it will bring it up. If you're
overcommitted, it will say so. And when it encourages you, it points at real evidence of your progress,
never empty cheerleading.

Setup — five steps, about ten minutes
1. Get Claude with Cowork
You need the Claude desktop app with Cowork mode (a Claude feature for working with your files
and apps). Download the app at claude.ai/download, sign in, and open a Cowork session. A paid
Claude plan is required.
2. Install the plugin
Drag the file chief-of-staff.plugin (included alongside this guide) into a Cowork chat and type:
"Please install this plugin." Claude will show a save/install button - click it. You can also add it from
Settings > Capabilities.
3. Connect Google Calendar and Gmail
In Claude's Settings, find Connectors and connect Google Calendar and Gmail, signing in with the
Google account you actually use day to day. This is what lets your Chief of Staff see your schedule
and inbox. (It can run without them, but this is where most of the value is.)
4. Pick a working folder
When Cowork asks which folder to work in, pick or create one (e.g., a folder called "Chief of Staff" in
Documents). The skill keeps its memory files there - plain text files you can open and read anytime.
5. Run setup
Type: cos setup - Claude will interview you for a few minutes: what to call you, when your energy
peaks, what your real priorities are, who you want to stay in touch with, and dates worth
remembering. Answer naturally; it stores the profile and you're done.
That's it. From now on, just talk to it.

Three habits that make it sing
Tell it what you intend to do.
Say things like "I'm going to finish the proposal Friday morning." It will pin the commitment to a time
slot and check whether it actually happened. This when-then technique is one of the best-evidenced
tools in behavioral psychology for follow-through.
Capture on the go.
Anywhere, anytime: email yourself with a subject line starting "cos:" (e.g., "cos: call the accountant
about Q3"). Next time you check in, it's already on your list.
Do a weekly review.
Once a week, ask "how did last week go." The honest gap between what you said and what you did is
where the system earns its keep.
Privacy and safety
Everything stays yours. The skill's memory lives in plain text files in the folder you chose - open them
anytime. It can read your calendar and email only through the connectors you approve, it never sends
email (only drafts that you send yourself), and it never creates or changes a calendar event without
showing you first and getting your yes. Events it creates are prefixed [CoS] so you can always spot
and remove them.
If something doesn't work
It says a connector isn't available
Check Settings > Connectors in Claude and reconnect Google Calendar or Gmail. The skill still works
without them, just with less visibility.
